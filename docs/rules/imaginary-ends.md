# Game Rules (Imaginary Ends)

Summary of the official rules for the Doomlings Imaginary Ends expansion, written in our own words as a reference for implementing the app. It adds to [Classic](classic.md). The official rulesheet is authoritative; if this file disagrees with it, the rulesheet wins.

Source: [rulesheet](../../assets/doomlings/rules/imaginary-ends.pdf) and its [transcription](../../assets/doomlings/rules/imaginary-ends.md).

## Components

- Deeplings: 16 traits plus 2 dominants, 2 ages, 1 catastrophe. They introduce suppress.
- Magical Merchants: 11 merchants and 40 trinkets.
- Glitterlings: 16 traits plus 2 dominants, 2 ages, 1 catastrophe. Cards about chance.
- Moonlings: 16 traits plus 2 dominants, 2 ages, 1 catastrophe. They give a lot away and do well with low Gene Pools.
- Fuzelings: 9 traits plus 1 dominant, 2 ages, 1 catastrophe. Combinations of Deeplings, Moonlings and Glitterlings, for bigger Drop of Life bonuses and new combos.

In all, the expansion has 64 traits (7 of them dominant), 8 ages and 4 catastrophes besides the merchants and trinkets.

The rulesheet recommends knowing Classic well first. Glitterlings and Moonlings come with no rules of their own beyond their cards.

## Suppress (Deeplings)

- A card with the suppress effect (it has its own icon) puts a card face down under a trait in your trait pile. Suppressing helps with smaller trait piles.
- Scoring: a suppressed card is not a trait. It has no face value and does not count for Drop of Life effects. Each is worth +1 point.
- Any number of cards may be suppressed under one trait, but not under a dominant.
- Like an action, if you cannot complete a suppress effect you may still play the trait, but you must complete it if you can. A suppress effect is not an action.
- Dominants: a dominant in your trait pile cannot be suppressed. A dominant in your hand can.
- Effects cannot interact with suppressed cards. When a trait leaves the trait pile, the cards suppressed under it go to the bottom of the trait deck.

## Magical Merchants

The goal is to pocket as many trinkets as you can, by meeting their objectives, to raise your score.

### Setup

- As in Classic, but each of the 3 age piles holds 1 basic age, 1 catastrophe and 2 merchants.
- Shuffle the trinkets face down into the trinket deck. After the traits are dealt, each player gains 2 trinkets, keeps 1 and trashes the other.
- A trashed trinket goes face up in the trash pile. When the trinket deck runs out, shuffle the trash pile into a new one. Running out of trinkets altogether is fine; play on as normal.

### Merchants

There are four merchants. They work much like ages and interact with trinkets.

### Trinkets

- A trinket has a pocket objective and a power that is active while it is face up.
- A trinket can be pocketed at any time by fulfilling its objective.
- At the start of an age, before its effects, each player without a face-up trinket gains 1.
- Pocketing: put the trinket face down next to your trait pile. It cannot be trashed or taken, and its points are yours.

Pocket objectives:

| Objective         | Fulfilled when                                                                               |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Play              | You play the required traits.                                                                |
| Reveal            | You show that you hold the required cards. With this objective you may reveal at any time.   |
| Hand Discard      | You discard the required cards from your hand, by effects or by stabilizing.                 |
| Trait Pile        | You have the required traits in your trait pile.                                             |
| Draw              | You draw the required cards, by effects or by stabilizing.                                   |
| Trait Pile Removal | The required traits are removed from your trait pile, for any reason.                       |

### World's End and Scoring

At World's End, all face-up trinkets are trashed. The points of pocketed trinkets are added to the final score.

### Conflicts

Trinket powers take priority over ages and trait effects.
