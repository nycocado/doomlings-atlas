## Overlush

Thanks for supporting Doomlings! We hope you enjoy the Overlush collection.

To recreate the Mystery Expansion experience, follow these steps:

Step 1:
Separate the basic traits, dominants, ages and catastrophes into 4 piles.

Step 2:
Shuffle each pile.

Step 3:
Draw 8 ages, 4 catastrophes, 8 dominants, and 40 traits.

Step 4:
Add these cards to the mix, set up your game as normal, and enjoy!

Or feel free to customize it to your liking. Consider drafting 15 dominants, and playing with plenty of classic Doomlings ages. However you decide to play, as long as you're having fun at the end of the world, that's what counts. And your points. They count too.
